Private by design

Your tree belongs to you.

Roots works without an account or cloud service. Your family information is stored locally on your device, and you decide when to import, export, or share it.

  • No trackingRoots does not collect or sell your family information.
  • No connection requiredBuild and explore your tree while offline.
  • You stay in controlMove your own data with roster and GEDCOM tools.

Better together, strong on their own

The companion app to Gide Legacy.

Roots organizes the people and relationships in your family. Gide Legacy preserves the memories and media that bring those people to life. Roots works fully on its own, and the two apps are designed to share one family roster when used together.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Does Roots require an account?

No. Roots works without an account, cloud service, or Google sign-in.

Can I import an existing family tree?

Yes. Roots supports GEDCOM import and export for compatible genealogy tools.

Is my family information uploaded?

No. Roots stores your family roster on your device. You decide if and when to share it.

Do I need Gide Legacy?

No. Roots is a standalone app, but it is designed as the companion app to Gide Legacy.
