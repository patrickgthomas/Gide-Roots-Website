GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions

Does Roots require an account?

No. Roots works without an account, cloud service, or Google sign-in.

Can I import an existing family tree?

Yes. Roots supports GEDCOM import and export for compatible genealogy tools.

Is my family information uploaded?

No. Roots stores your family roster on your device. You decide if and when to share it.

Do I need Gide – Legacy?

No. Roots is a standalone app, but it is designed as the companion app to Gide – Legacy.
